# Published content metadata

Astro Markdown articles remain in `src/content/articles/` and are validated by `src/content.config.ts`. Existing public articles may carry `sourceType`, `sourceId` or `sourceRevision`; these are **historical provenance**, not a live Google Sheets/Notion/Obsidian connector.

The old publishing integrations have been removed. Keep the existing published article URLs and content unchanged.
